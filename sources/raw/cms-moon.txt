Medicare Outpatient Observation Notice
Important! You’re getting this notice because your hospital status is “hospital outpatient
receiving observation services,” not “hospital inpatient.”
This means your hospital stay will be billed to Medicare Part B instead of Part A.

x

When you’re a hospital outpatient, your observation stay is covered under Medicare Part B. This means you’ll
have a copayment/coinsurance for each outpatient hospital service you get.

x

Your Part B copayments/coinsurance may be lower or higher than the Part A inpatient deductible. Your hospital
can give you more information about billing.

x

After you leave the hospital, Medicare may not pay if you go to a skilled nursing facility.
