## Detailed Explanation of Non-coverage

Date: March 12, 2025
Patient name: Walter Pruitt

This notice explains why your provider and/or health plan decided Medicare coverage for your current services should end. This notice is not the decision on your appeal. The decision on your appeal will come from your Quality Improvement Organization (QIO).

## Why your services are no longer covered

We reviewed your case and decided that Medicare coverage of your home health services should end.

- The facts used to make this decision: Your nurse's visit notes from March 3 and March 10, 2025 show your surgical wound has closed, and you no longer need daily wound care.
- Detailed explanation of why your services are no longer covered, and the Medicare coverage rules used to make this decision: Medicare covers home health care only while you need skilled nursing or therapy on an intermittent basis. Your wound care can now be done by you or a caregiver, so you no longer need skilled nursing. Rule used: Medicare Benefit Policy Manual, Chapter 7, Section 40.1.
- Specific plan policy used to make the decision (health plans only): Not applicable. You have Original Medicare.

To get a copy of the rules or guidelines used to make this decision, or a copy of the documents sent to the QIO, call us at 1-866-555-0129.
